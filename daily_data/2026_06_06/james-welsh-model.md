## The Six Rules of the James Welsh Model

Distilled patterns observable across all five sampled videos:

### 1. Teacher Tone, Never Promoter Tone
He doesn't say "obsessed", "holy grail", "you NEED this". He says "tricky", "really decent", "for those who want…". Permission-giving language, not urgency language.

### 2. Self-Disqualifier in the Hook
Multiple paid videos open with what the product is *not* for. "My teenage skin would've loved" = signals you may not be the target. Counterintuitively this raises trust.

### 3. Category Naming, Not Feature Hyping
"Typical kbeauty stick balm" frames the product as one of a category, with you the viewer trusted to compare. Contrast to Chloe Ferry's "INSANE bundle deal!" which assumes you can't compare.

### 4. Skepticism Acknowledged Before Endorsement
"Experts still debate" + "I still use" is the credibility trick. He concedes ground before claiming any. Audience reads this as the dermatologist-level honesty H8 predicted.

### 5. Reply-Based Audience Recruitment
Replying to @trishapaytas (massive non-skincare celeb) put 135k strangers in front of his K-skincare opinion. He uses celebrity reply as audience-acquisition, not sponsorship.

### 6. Paid Content That Looks Like Education
All three paid videos in our sample are framed as advice ("for those who want…", "if you have X skin…"). The product is the subject of a lesson, not the lesson itself. Audience can't tell where teaching ends and selling begins.

## What This Means for Ireland 18-34 Strategy

### The 100:1 Rule
If we ship 1 paid IE K-skincare video, we should have shipped ~100 unpaid/educational ones first. Otherwise the paid content reads as a stranger asking for trust. Realistic interim: 1 paid for every 10 organic, scaling toward 1:50+ over time.

### Voice Calibration
Drop these from any IE script:
- "obsessed", "holy grail", "you NEED this", "INSANE", "10/10"
- ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Hard CTAs ("link in bio", "30% off")

Adopt these:
- "really decent", "a nice one for", "tricky", "this works if you have X"
- "experts still debate", "I'm still not 100% sure but"
- Soft CTAs ("if you want to look it up", "I've linked the receipt")

### Format Template (replicable in IE accent)
- **Hook**: Reply to a non-skincare moment in the news/celeb sphere, OR self-disqualifier ("this isn't for everyone, but…")
- **Body**: 30-60 seconds of category-level teaching, product as example not subject
- **End**: A concession (acknowledged uncertainty) + soft pointer

### Brand Partnership Implication
We should look for the **VT / Anua / Mixsoon-shaped brands** for IE seeding — second-tier K-brands that need credibility-first creators, not first-tier brands that buy mass paid reach. medicube and Dr.Melaxin have Chloe Ferry; mid-tier brands need the James Welsh layer.

### Creator Recruitment Filter
The Irish James Welsh archetype we should hunt for:
- 100-500k followers
- Subject-matter authority (skin, beauty, dermatology, pharmacy)
- 70%+ female audience
- 4%+ ER
- Posts 3-5×/week, has been posting 2+ years
- No more than 5% of posts are clearly sponsored

This is a very specific filter. Probably 0-3 creators in Ireland fit it today.
