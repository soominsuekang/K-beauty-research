The **gap to close**: ingredient discourse (PDRN, exosomes, "bloom skin" replacing "glass skin"). The **gap that's already closed**: physical access and SPF velocity.

## UK-IE gap analysis (2-3 paragraphs)

**The retail gap has closed faster than the briefing implied.** Yesterday's eod-briefing framed Boots IE + Arnotts + Brown Thomas + Planet Beauty as "enough IE physical retail to ground content." That undersold the picture. With **Sissel Lab Dublin** running as Ireland's first dedicated K-beauty specialist (Stephen's Green, opened July 2025, BOJ/Anua/Medicube/Round Lab confirmed and "more brands on the way"), **K-Beauty Cult in Galway City** running an indie specialist with bike delivery, and **Penneys' own-label PS… K-Beauty line** launched at the Dublin SS26 preview, Ireland now has a four-tier retail stack — dedicated specialist, dept-store edit, mass pharmacy, fast-fashion own-label — that mirrors the UK's depth rather than lagging it. The phrase "imported from the UK" no longer describes IE K-beauty in 2026.

**Where Ireland still trails is the ingredient-credibility conversation.** The UK has named protagonists in the PDRN debate (Dr Aamna Adel endorsing, Dr Paris Acharya pushing back, Grazia panels weighing in). Ireland has the *retail* to back the same conversation but does not yet have an IE-named dermatologist voice on the public PDRN ledger. The Institute of Dermatologists' Caitríona Ryan and Niki Ralph already curate a "Skincare Favourites" list — there is no K-beauty product on it as of this report. That's the strategic opening: the **first IE-dermatologist who publicly takes a side on PDRN, ideally with a Boots-IE or Sissel Lab-stocked product in hand, owns the IE conversation by default** because no one else is in the seat.

**Format gap**: UK content has migrated from haul videos to verdict videos (per yesterday's briefing); IE creator output is still skewed toward access-and-discovery format (the @leomoonstone Sissel Lab walk-through being the archetype). That's the next 30-day rotation we should drive: convert IE creator briefs from "look what just arrived" to "I tested both on my skin and one loses" — using only products on the Sissel Lab/Boots IE/Arnotts shelf. The constraint is the content engine, not a limit on it.
