## Pattern of error

**Three systematic over-confidences emerge this week:**

1. **Date conflation between "announcement / soft-trade" and "grand opening"** (Pureseoul 22 May vs 19 June). The routine treats first-press-mention as the live date. **Mitigation**: every retail-anchor brief should carry a "press-announce date" AND "live date" field, never collapsed.

2. **Country-of-origin / "Asian beauty" over-attribution** (Sephora Belfast Tatcha + Yepoda framed as K-brands). The routine is treating "Asian-inspired" as synonymous with "K-beauty" in some adjacencies. **Mitigation**: maintain a Korea-strict brand whitelist; when a retailer-mix is cited as "K-beauty," each brand must be on the whitelist or marked "J-coded" / "European K-inspired" / "K-American."

3. **Trend-report attribution drift** (Bloom Skin → Olive Young vs Refinery29 + April Brodie). Coverage-chain laundering: a UK facialist's quote becomes a Korean retail-giant's official 2026 stance within 3-4 outlets. **Mitigation**: for any "Korea retired X / Korea named Y" line, demand a direct Korean-source URL (Olive Young Korea press, K-MOFA, Cosmo Beauty Seoul primary survey). The routine has the rigour to enforce this — Day-13 Cosmo Beauty Seoul survey is the model.

**Non-finding worth noting**: dermatologist credentials + retail SKUs + brand-ambassador-deal dates this week were **highly accurate**. The routine's spine on the people / product / launch axes is trustworthy; the leakage is on **derived framings** (date collapse, category attribution, source-chain).
