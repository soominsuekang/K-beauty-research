Format: 12-15s, hold the product reveal until "she loves me," cut on "she loves me not."

"The Cure" snippets are the high-leverage pre-drop entry today (lyric maps directly to a cica / barrier-repair angle); on Friday-Saturday the album-snippet volume fragments and "Drop Dead" reclaims the dominant lane, so re-use the Thursday cut on Friday with a "skin has never looked better" overlay.
