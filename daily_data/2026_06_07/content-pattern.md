## Content strategy notes
*3 actionable for IE 18-34 women:*

1. **Brief two organic templates, not one.** Yesterday's note said "commission chatty-life GRWM." Today's data shows the GRWM single-author outlier (ElleMoonz) didn't repeat — but the **two-mode pattern** did: (a) ≤20s talking-head confession with "anyone else do this?" energy, (b) 120–210s long-form store-tour or celebrity-reply. Brief both. The short version is a Frishta-style mirror-confession with a one-line "anyone else…" caption. The long version is an iona francis-style "Top 5 Korean serums for [Irish skin / Galway weather / busy-mum routine]" verdict-list, 60–120s. Both work organic; both can name 4+ K-beauty brands per video. The iona francis pattern is NEW today and is the cheapest to operationalise — it's a slideshow + VO over flatlay, can be cut in an hour.

2. **Sound = original audio + auto-captions, NEVER a trending pop snippet for organic.** 100% of today's top 12 organic videos use spoken VO. Zero of them used Beat Automotivo Tan Tan Tan or the other top-3 UK trending sounds — which is counterintuitive given the popular_music_uk file shows those at 33M+ weekly plays. The trending-sound playbook is for paid montage / haul (where ER doesn't matter, reach does). For an IE 18-34 creator brief that's trying to build organic ER, the directive is: talking-head, captions burned-in, no song behind it. Cost implication: skip music licensing entirely.

3. **Do NOT replicate the Boots UK retailer-tag template.** Three different paid creators ran "at Boots UK" + invisible-mask / "the only skin prep you need" in the past 28 days. ER averages 0.16% (worst paid cluster in the pool). The retailer-tag works when it's *organic surprise* (Graces.faces "I'm back in Costco" = 7.76% ER) and dies when it's *paid placement* (Sydney Purl @ Boots = 0.28% ER, Chelsea Thomas Irwin @ Boots = 0.04% ER). If a partner retailer (Boots IE, Arnotts, McCabes) wants creator content for IE, do not let them dictate "tag the store" — let the creator pick the retail moment. The Costco surprise > the Boots transaction every time.
