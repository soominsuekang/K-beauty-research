## Strategy notes
*3 actionable for IE 18-34 women:*

1. **Brief the chatty-life GRWM, not the chatty #gurwm.** ElleMoonz's "Alexander's first day of school" at 16.8% ER is the new high-water mark for grwm in our pool. It works because the *life occasion* (kid's first school day) anchors the brand-list-in-caption, not the products. IE equivalents: school-run grwm, Leaving Cert results-day grwm, first-day-of-new-job grwm, Galway Races weekend grwm. The product list must include 4+ K-beauty SKUs but the *hook* is the life moment. Day-1's #gurwm hypothesis is shaky after today's zero-result pull — pivot the format brief to story-arc GRWM with Biodance/MEDIHEAL named in caption alongside non-K-beauty staples (Clinique, The Ordinary) for authenticity.

2. **Commission the "ex-Superdrug worker reveals secret launches" Irish equivalent immediately.** Graces.faces's second store-tour today (125k views, 6.83% ER, named Biodance + medicube + TIRTIR + anua) confirms store-tour is a *franchise*, not a single-video format. The Irish version writes itself: ex-Boots Henry Street worker, ex-Arnotts beauty hall worker, or ex-Sissel Lab employee revealing "secret launches" — same intimacy hook, same insider-knowledge frame, same multi-brand caption-list. Production cost <€500 per video, owns an uncontested IE cluster. **Priority: book one creator outreach this week.**

3. **Stop chasing Dr.Melaxin paid slots; chase Biodance organic mentions.** Today's data shows two crossings: (a) BOJ entering paid (Nath Henry × 2, low ER), (b) Biodance entering organic (ElleMoonz 16.8% ER, Graces.faces store-tour 6.83% ER). Dr.Melaxin remains 100% paid with 0 organic afterlife — every euro spent on Dr.Melaxin paid extension is captive. Biodance's pattern is the inverse: brand is becoming a credible name-drop in chatty creator content, which means IE creators can mention Biodance organically without it reading as #ad. **The IE creator brief should treat Biodance as a "name in passing" brand and Dr.Melaxin as a "do not mention" brand for the first two weeks of organic content** — exactly the opposite of what the FastMoss top-promoted leaderboard would suggest.
