By June 7 there is **still zero organic Mamonde surface** in the FastMoss organic 12. - **Dr.Melaxin: 100% paid, 0 organic for 7+ days running.** Yesterday's call was "no organic afterlife"; today's call is unchanged. **Lag: undefined; the momentum signal never crosses paid → organic threshold for Dr.Melaxin.** This is itself the strongest day-over-day pattern (a negative one).

**Running estimate (Day 1 baseline)**: in this UK K-skincare cohort, the *paid-video-to-organic-mention* lag for SKUs that DO eventually convert appears to sit at ~4-6 weeks (Biodance is the cleanest n=1).
