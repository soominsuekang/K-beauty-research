- **Medicube "Super Brand Week" is live on TikTok Shop UK and dominating our viral signal.** The AGE-R Booster Pro X2 (launched in UK 11 Jun at ~£230) is being pushed via voucher stacks down to ~£121, and multiple AD-labeled UK creator videos (Makeup by Katy A, ZhilaBeauty, ALLAN ⚡️, Hannah St Luce, XTHUYLE) appear in our `viral_kbeauty_7d` top 10 — every one of them carries `#medicubesuperbrandweek` or `#medicubeagerbooster`. *Why it matters:* Medicube is paying creators harder than any other K-brand right now in the UK. The £121 price point reframes "premium device" content as "summer-sale impulse buy" — that's a different content shape than the £200+ "investment piece" frame we discussed last week, and it's easier for our 18-34 IE audience to act on.
- **Pureseoul opened its 16th UK store at SouthGate Bath on 19 June** — first South-West UK location, with 5 more under construction and 3 more leases signed for summer. *Why it matters for us:* Pureseoul is becoming the UK's anchor K-beauty physical retailer. They stock Torriden, RealBarrier, Sungboon — not our priority shortlist directly, but they're a search/discovery hub our IE audience uses. Bath opening is a non-Dublin/Belfast story but the doubling-of-store-count trajectory is an editorial peg for a "K-beauty IRL: where to buy in UK&IE 2026" piece later this summer.
- **COSRX-led Amazon UK Prime Day push (23–26 Jun, up to 63% off)** is brand-side promo, not launch — Snail 92 All-In-One Cream, Vit C 23 serum, PDRN Exosome Skinplaning Glaze Mask are the headlined discounts. A "K-beauty Prime Day picks" video for IE audiences (Amazon.ie ships same deals) is a low-effort, high-relevance content slot — and COSRX is the only one of our priority brands actively running a Prime Day storyline this week.
- **Anua Manchester pop-up opens in 4 days (29 Jun)** at Selfridges Trafford Centre — runs to 12 July, the brand's final UK Selfridges stop. PDRN Mist + Lip Serum + Skin Analyser tool + £30+ spend gift bundle continue from Birmingham/London format. *Why it matters:* this is Anua's last "in-person, IRL, brand-built UK moment" before brand attention dilutes for the rest of summer.
