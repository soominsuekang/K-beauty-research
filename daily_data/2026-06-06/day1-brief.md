## TL;DR

After analyzing 7 FastMoss exports, 100 Most Promoted crawl rows, and 200+ Viral Video rows across 3 time windows (7d/28d/90d) and 2 keywords ('korean skincare' + 'kbeauty'):

1. **K-skincare = 80-87% of UK TikTok skincare market** (medicube + Dr.Melaxin dominate).
2. **Paid promotion ≠ sales**. Spearman correlation ~0. Wonderskin spent 2,700 affiliates → £3.3k revenue. INKEY List won without paid push.
3. **Time-window choice flips the conclusion**: 7d = 94% AD visibility (paid wins short-term gaze), 28d = organic wins (4x views, 8x ER), 90d = paid catches up via long-tail.
4. **5 winning organic formats** identified: STORE-TOUR, CELEBRITY-REPLY, VERDICT/BEFORE-AFTER, #gurwm, DEBATE-style skepticism.
5. **Vocabulary shift confirmed**: 'anti-aging' → 'well-ageing'/'skin longevity'. Boots 2026 Trends Report makes NAD+ official.
6. **One mixed-strategy master found**: @james_s_welsh — his paid videos get organic-tier ER (1.5-3.3%). This is THE model for IE strategy.
7. **Ireland retail signal strong**: Boots IE K-Beauty Box sold out 24h. Boots Henry Street, Arnotts, Brown Thomas all stocking K-skincare. **IE TikTok creator vacuum exists** — strategic opening.

## Methodology Recap (for routine context)

### Data sources used today
- **FastMoss Top Selling**: Monthly All/Beauty/Skincare Top 50 (3 Excel files). Weekly All Top 1-10 (2 files), Beauty Top 50, Skincare Top 50.
- **FastMoss Most Promoted** (partial crawl, blocked by captcha): All Monthly Top 50, Beauty Top 30, Skincare Top 20.
- **FastMoss Viral Videos** (crawled): Beauty category, 7-day window 50 videos × 1 keyword, 28-day 50 × 2 keywords (korean skincare + kbeauty), 90-day 50 × 1 keyword. ≈ 200 unique videos.
- **External**: First eod-briefing (web-fetched UK/IE news, Reddit, public TikTok signals).

### Sample limits to remember
- Top 50 only per window (long-tail invisible).
- Single category filter (Beauty `cid=4`), no L2 sub-category.
- Searched in 2 keywords; #gurwm, #glassskin, #pdrn etc. not directly queried.
- Organic videos = 11 across combined 28d sample → small n, hypotheses are DIRECTIONAL not statistical.
- All viewed videos UK-based; no IE-tagged creators surfaced in viral pool.

## Twelve Hypotheses (H1-H12) — Tracking Status

### Original 8 (Day 1 seed)

**H1**: Verdict-format videos (BOJ vs medicube vs Anua, named loser) outperform single-product hauls 4:1.
- **Status**: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED. Only 2/50 (4%) videos in 7d sample contained verdict keywords. 90d sample had Mixsoon before/after (Amy Burnside 111k views). Verdict format viral but uncommon in current pool.

**H2**: 'Anti-aging' vocab dying; 'wellageing' / 'skin longevity' replacing.
- **Status**: SUPPORTED. Boots 2026 Trends Report explicit. iona francis still using 'anti-aging' in caption ('Top 5 Korean serums for anti-aging') — vocab transition in flight, not complete.

**H3**: Organic videos achieve 4x views, 8x ER vs paid in 28d window.
- **Status**: STRONGLY SUPPORTED. 28d data: Org avg views 218k vs paid 54k. Org median ER 6.11% vs paid 0.71%.

**H4**: STORE-TOUR is highest-leverage organic angle.
- **Status**: SUPPORTED. Graces.faces_ Costco 207-second video = 653k views, 7.76% ER (single top organic across all samples).

**H5**: CELEBRITY-REPLY gets cheap traffic.
- **Status**: SUPPORTED. James Welsh @ Trisha Paytas = 135k views, 5.07% ER. Christina Mitsi 'Replying to @Pizza' = 1.3M (paid, but reply hook drove views). User-reply also viral.

**H6**: #gurwm is emerging format with high ER.
- **Status**: SUPPORTED (small sample). Cindy Lee #gurwm 15.4k views, 8.54% ER — top ER in entire 95-video sample.

**H7**: PDRN ingredient debate is content gold.
- **Status**: STRONGLY SUPPORTED. PDRN ingredient itself: organic views 476k vs paid 139k (3.4x organic premium). Dr.Melaxin = 100% paid push (0 organic); medicube = mixed (Frishta 446k organic for medicube products).

**H8**: Dermatologist-creator is new trust gate (Dr Aamna Adel, Dr Paris Acharya).
- **Status**: SUPPORTED qualitatively. PDRN credibility war in motion. Ireland likely lacks equivalent — strategic opening.

### New 4 (added 2026-06-06)

**H9 (NEW)**: 'korean skincare' = organic-driven keyword; 'kbeauty' = paid-driven. IE content should hashtag 'korean skincare' for organic ER lift.
- **Status**: SUPPORTED. 28d data: 'korean skincare' Org avg ER 5.98% vs 'kbeauty' Org avg ER 3.37%. AD share identical (88%) but organic quality differs.

**H10 (NEW)**: List format ("Top 5 Korean X") works organically even for smaller channels.
- **Status**: SUPPORTED (weakly, n=2). iona francis (132k followers) got 16k+11k views ER 2.3-2.75% with 'Top 5 Korean serums for anti-aging' / 'Top 5 Korean retinoids'. Low barrier entry format.

**H11 (NEW)**: Mixed-strategy creators (paid that feels organic) are the most stable performers. James Welsh is the model.
- **Status**: STRONGLY SUPPORTED (single dominant example). His paid ER 1.52-3.27% (5x typical paid creator). Mixed AD/Organic ratio with consistent voice across both.

**H12 (NEW)**: "Companion intimacy" persona ('I'm doing my skincare while filming, you can do this faster') drives ER 7%+.
- **Status**: SUPPORTED (n=1). ZhilaBeauty (1.3M followers): 68.5k views, 7.31% ER on '5-min skincare while filming' video. Needs more data points.

## Day 1 Closing Note

The biggest single insight today: **the choice of time window flips the answer**. A content strategy built only on 7-day viral data would conclude "you must do paid promotion or you won't be visible." A strategy built only on 90-day data would conclude "paid actually wins long-term via cumulative tail." The truth lives in the 28-day window: organic dominates when given time to breathe.

For our Ireland 18-34 strategy: **content that doesn't need paid amplification to survive 28 days = the only sustainable model**. That maps directly to James Welsh's playbook (teacher tone, debate-acknowledgment, no hard CTA) and Graces.faces's playbook (store-tour, long-form, price-shock hook).

Tomorrow's routine cycle should refine all 12 hypotheses. The most important re-check is H3 (organic dominance in 28d) and H11 (James Welsh as mixed-strategy model). H6 (#gurwm) and H12 (companion intimacy) need more data points before promoting confidence.
