# Creator Persona Cards — Ireland K-Skincare Content Strategy

**Date**: 2026-06-06
**Purpose**: Templates to copy + Ireland creator vacuum mapping.

---

## Summary of findings

After analyzing 5 UK organic-strong creator profiles via FastMoss + Favikon Top 20 IE Beauty list:

### Critical pattern discovered: brand-creator split
**5 of 5 UK organic-strong creators (James Welsh, ZhilaBeauty, Graces.faces_, Frishta, Cindy Lee) feature the same K-brand portfolio: VT / Anua / Mixsoon.**

None of them work with medicube or Dr.Melaxin (the dominant paid-push brands). This is not a coincidence — it is a structural split in the K-skincare UK market:

- **Paid-push tier**: medicube, Dr.Melaxin → Chloe Ferry, Nath Henry, Stephanie Vavron (ER 0.5-0.8%)
- **Organic-friendly tier**: VT, Anua, Mixsoon → James Welsh, Graces.faces_, ZhilaBeauty (ER 5-8%)

For our Ireland strategy, this means: **if we are seeding IE content via brand partnership, target VT / Anua / Mixsoon / Numbuzin / Biodance — not medicube / Dr.Melaxin.**

### Ireland creator vacuum confirmed
Favikon Top 20 Beauty Ireland 2026 has **zero** K-skincare-specialized creators. Top tier is mostly makeup artists (Kash Beauty, Sculpted by Aimee, LMD Cosmetics, Mrs Glam founders). Only Carrie MacMahon (#18, 8,171 pts) lists "skincare" in focus area at all.

→ **Ireland K-skincare TikTok creator role is uncontested.**

## Strategic recommendation summary

### For IE content creation (if we are making content directly)
Best starting persona: **#5 (Cindy Lee, micro-moment #gurwm)** — lowest production cost, single sentence, IE accent works immediately. Test 5 videos in this format first.

Best high-leverage persona: **#2 (Graces.faces_, store-tour)** — once we identify a creator willing to walk Boots Henry Street / Arnotts K-Beauty shelf in IE accent, single video has 100k+ ceiling.

### For IE partnership (if we are seeding via brand)
Best targets to approach: **Carrie MacMahon, Aoife, Aideen Kate, ASTA Jurksaite** — Tier A candidates who already have engaged audiences and adjacent focus.

Brand partners to bring with you: **VT, Anua, Mixsoon, Numbuzin, Biodance** — second-tier K-brands with proven organic-friendly creator approach, not medicube/Dr.Melaxin.

### Strategic opening (untouched white space)
**No IE-based dermatologist or clinical-authority K-skincare creator exists.** This is the Dr Aamna Adel role. Seeding this role (recruit an IE pharmacist or dermatologist into TikTok) is the highest long-term leverage move.
