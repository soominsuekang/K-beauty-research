## UK-IE gap analysis (2-3 paragraphs)

**Today's headline shift is from "stock parity" to "disclosed pipeline parity."** Yesterday's report established that IE K-beauty retail had geographic parity with the UK across Dublin–Cork–Galway. Today extends both axes simultaneously: geographic (Planet Beauty's Limerick footprint adds a fourth IE city tier, putting K-Beauty inside a department-store carrier in Limerick for the first time) and roadmap (Arnotts' first-party "Coming Soon: TirTir, Kaja, Fwee, Anua" pre-announcement is the first time an IE retailer has publicly disclosed a future K-brand pipeline rather than restocking quietly). The IE K-beauty retail tier is now visibly running its own merchandising plans rather than mirroring Boots UK with a lag. TirTir's cushion line and Fwee's lip line, in particular, take IE K-beauty *out* of pure skincare into makeup-adjacency for the first time at the dept-store level — a category extension that has been the UK trajectory for 6 months and now has a confirmed IE entry point.

**The K-Pharmacy frame has moved from theory to a credentialed five-chain pattern, with Meaghers' Seoulista exclusivity as the new credibility lever.** Boots UK's 2026 Trends Report flagged K-Pharmacy as a 2026 vertical extension; IE already has Cara, dPharmacy, Mellericks Cork running de facto K-pharmacy. Today adds Meaghers Pharmacy (Dublin-HQ, multi-store) with both a standing Korean Skincare collection page *and* exclusive carriage of Seoulista Beauty, a Seoul-sourced K-brand that historically lived only in UK mass-pharmacy. IE-owned pharmacy is now first to a K-brand the UK pharmacy network does not own — a quiet credentialing flip that goes the other way to the usual UK → IE lag. The negative print is Hickey's Pharmacy: a major IE chain (25+ years, Irish-owned community pharmacy network) that still has no K-beauty category page or named K-brand. Hickey's is the single biggest "open lane" left in IE pharmacy retail — either a competitive pressure they'll need to close, or a partnership opening for a K-brand seeking a 5th IE pharmacy distribution rail.

**The cross-border Sephora NI moment is a 2027 watch-item that already changes 2026 acquisition behaviour.** Sephora Belfast opened 12 February 2026 (2,550 sq ft, Victoria Square), and Sephora UK has confirmed it will receive the Olive Young K-beauty zone in the 2027 wave (US/Canada/HK/SEA 2026 → UK/ME/Australia 2027). The strategic implication for IE: Republic-of-Ireland consumers within 2-hour drive of Belfast (Dublin, Drogheda, Dundalk, Monaghan, Cavan) already have a non-Boots, non-Arnotts, non-specialist Sephora-format K-beauty option *now*, and that option will absorb the full Olive Young assortment 12–18 months before any ROI retailer can match it. This makes the Dublin–Belfast cross-border consumer the audience that will set the IE category's premium reference price; ROI retailers (Boots IE, Arnotts, Brown Thomas) have ~18 months to lock in IE-specific differentiation before Sephora-by-proxy becomes the IE consumer's default mental price anchor.
