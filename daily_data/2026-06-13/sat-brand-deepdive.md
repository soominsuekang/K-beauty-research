## Strategic read for our IE 18-34 strategy

### Should we use this brand?

**Yes — with caveats.** Specifically: **yes for hero-product mentions in multi-brand routine / store-tour / fact-check / verdict formats. No for a dedicated single-brand "I tried medicube for 30 days" piece** (that frame is saturated by UK paid-creator output).

**Why yes:**

1. **Only paid-push brand with proven organic spillover.** Every other paid-push brand (Dr.Melaxin, Erborian, Mamonde, Smuuti Skin UK) is paid-only. medicube is **dual-tier**.
2. **IE retail depth is exceptional** — 10 stockists across 4 retail tiers; easier to film and easier to viewer-verify than e.g. Numbuzin or BOJ (which are concentrated at fewer stockists).
3. **Boots IE €40 K-Box reconciliation** is an open content opportunity — physical Henry St walk-in, on-camera, resolves it for the audience.
4. **Sissel Lab specialist credibility** — medicube on the dedicated K-beauty specialist's shelf is a stronger trust signal than mass-pharmacy presence.
5. **The pink-skincare "I caved" comment pattern** is a ready-made creator hook — reluctance-then-conversion is content gold for the 22-28 cluster.

**Why with caveats:**

1. **Brand is over-distributed; brand-saturation flag is live.** Two SKUs in UK TT Shop top 5; 4 SKUs in top-10 promoted. Mentioning medicube does not differentiate; it's table-stakes K-beauty literacy.
2. **AD content for medicube is the lowest-ER paid format in our pool** (Chloe Ferry 0.50%, Vavron 0.67%, Welsh **1.30% on a 5.58% baseline**). Any IE paid post would inherit this drag.
3. **The James Welsh ER collapse** is a leading signal that K-content fatigue may be hitting medicube hardest among the paid-push set. Watch before committing to any UK→IE creator brief with medicube as the lead brand.
