## UK-IE gap analysis (2-3 paragraphs)

**Today's headline shift is from "shelf parity" to "rail parity."** The IE category had stock parity (Day 2), geographic parity (Day 3), and disclosed-pipeline parity (Day 3). What it didn't have yet was channel-and-subscription parity with the UK. TikTok Shop Ireland's full open-access announcement (Katey McElroy at the Dublin Creator Matchmaking event, with Guaranteed Ireland) closes the channel half: any IE K-stockist — SkinShop, Sissel Lab, Cloud10, Meaghers, Kawaii Gal — can now run a creator-affiliate K-beauty campaign through TikTok Shop IE without invite gating, and the 13 IE TikTok creators on our tracker already form a usable distribution roster. Kawaii Gal's €34.99 mystery-box subscription closes the subscription half: the IE consumer can now subscribe to a recurring K+J education loop without depending on a Boots-IE LE box drop. The UK had both rails for 18+ months; IE got both in the same week.

**The BOJ Daily Tinted Fluid Sunscreen launch + Boots-UK first-party leaderboard is the most actionable single signal of the cycle.** Boots-UK has now confirmed in its own newsroom that Beauty of Joseon is the best-selling Korean beauty brand at Boots and that BOJ Relief Sun: Rice + Probiotics is the best-selling K-beauty product at Boots — full stop, no qualifications. The new launch (Daily Tinted Fluid Sunscreen, Boots-exclusive) extends BOJ's sunscreen flagship into the tinted/hybrid-makeup space, which is the same makeup-adjacency category Arnotts is opening with TirTir cushions and Fwee lips. IE read-across is high-confidence: Boots IE already carries the BOJ category page, and the Sissel Lab Stephen's Green BOJ Relief Sun stockout pattern just finished its 4th week — a tinted variant would slot in at velocity-tier from day one. The 2–4 week IE mirror window is the highest-leverage content peg of the cycle.

**The IE category now has its own demand-side leaderboard inheritable from UK and two delivery-side rails the UK does not particularly own (Kawaii Gal as IE-headquartered, TikTok Shop IE as creator-roster-native).** Note the asymmetry: Boots-UK is the demand validator (BOJ = #1) but Kawaii Gal is *headquartered* in Ireland and serves UK from there, and TikTok Shop IE's creator roster includes Portuguese/Spanish/Brazilian-IE Dublin voices (@mayviles, @bycarolinerocha, Daniela Ruiz) that the UK domestic roster does not have. For the first time in this 14-day track, IE has structural advantages — multilingual creator depth, founder-story credibility, IE-headquartered subscription mechanic — that the UK domestic register cannot replicate by simple read-across. The strategic frame shifts from "IE is UK with a lag" to "IE inherits UK demand but owns differentiated mechanics."
