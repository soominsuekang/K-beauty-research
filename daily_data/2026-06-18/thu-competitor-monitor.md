## Notable brand moves this week

- **Medicube launched AGE-R "Booster Pro X2" home device in the UK on 11 June** via TikTok Shop UK + Amazon UK simultaneously with US. APR's press push is unmistakably trying to lift Medicube from consumable-skincare brand to device-brand. *Why it matters for us:* device tier is a different content shape than serum/pad reviews — if we lean into Medicube, we now have a £200+ premium SKU we can pitch as "investment piece" content versus the £17 pad. Margin and pay potential is higher.
- **Anua named Kendall Jenner global ambassador (1 Jun) AND is mid-UK Selfridges pop-up tour:** Birmingham wrapped 14 Jun (this week), Manchester runs 29 Jun–12 Jul. The Birmingham pop-up offered a UK-exclusive PDRN Lip Serum + keyring set. *Why it matters:* Anua is at peak heat in the UK right now. We should batch Anua content (PDRN serum spray, lip serum, mist) in next 14 days while creator/press push is live and search demand is up. After 12 Jul the moment cools.
- **Numbuzin No.9 NAD Eye Patches at Boots, riding "NAD+ as 2026 wonder ingredient" trend that Boots itself called out in its 2026 Trends Report.** *Why it matters:* this is a brand we're already shortlisted on, and Boots is actively merchandising the trend. Editorial fit for Ireland audience (Boots IE stocks Numbuzin) and content angle "what is NAD+" is currently under-supplied on UK TikTok.
- **Beauty of Joseon is conserving firepower for an Aug 11 OOH London takeover with Peggy Gou** (Piccadilly, Oxford Circus, Covent Garden, Carnaby). *Why it matters:* a pre-take seeding window opens late July. The brand is going quieter through June-July intentionally, which means our June BoJ content competes less and ranks easier, but won't have brand-side amplification — schedule heavier BoJ content for early August.
- **Dr.Melaxin keeps pouring affiliate spend through TikTok Shop UK** (CACTOX V-Lifting Band is the #1 promoted skincare SKU in our fastmoss_raw monthly file at £174k GMV, 1.3k affiliates), but parallel TikTok backlash content tagged "Dr.Melaxin scam," "Dr.Melaxin bad reviews," "chemical burns" is growing. *Why it matters:* validates our Day-1 position to avoid Dr.Melaxin organic. If they DM us a paid offer, we'd need to gate it on QC.
- **FDA approved Bemotrizinol on 9 June** — the UV filter that Beauty of Joseon's flagship sunscreen relies on. UK formulation is unaffected (UK already allows it), but the global narrative ("the K-sunscreen filter is finally legal in America") is a content hook we can localize for IE/UK audiences as "why your BoJ sunscreen is the original formula, unlike US version."

Use the FDA Bemotrizinol news (9 Jun) as the one near-term BoJ hook ("the K-sunscreen filter that just got approved in the US — but we've had it for years in IE").

An organic endorsement risks our credibility; a paid endorsement should be vetted, sample-tested, and explicitly labeled.
