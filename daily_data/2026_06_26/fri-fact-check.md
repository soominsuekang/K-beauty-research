## Pattern of error

Two systematic over-confidences detected this week:

1. **Credential inflation.** The system inflates skincare creators into medical professionals: James Welsh repeatedly called "dermatologist" / "dermatologist-led" across 4 daily briefings. There is no internal check that the underlying creator actually holds the credential being attributed. Other named creators with implicit-expert framing (Pharmacist Edel Lannons, Dr Edel Woods, Dr Anjali Mahto, Dr Sam Bunting, Charlotte Palermino, Hyram) were NOT audited this week and should be on next week's list.

2. **Date / year projection on entertainment-vertical anchor events.** The sounds + cultural-anchor routine appears to be presenting 2025 events (F1 movie, F1 Album, ROSÉ "Messy") as if they are imminent 2026 events — likely because the model is pattern-matching from training data where these were the "current" entertainment moment, without an internal check that the calendar has moved. CORTIS REDRED (a genuinely 2026 event) was correctly tagged, so the issue is selective rather than uniform.

3. **Reconciliation invention.** When two real numbers conflict (Anua 470 vs 650 Boots stores), the briefing invents a definitional split to harmonise them rather than recognising they are sequential snapshots. Forces a fake "open debate" into the carry-forward.

4. **Single-source quantitative anchors are not flagged.** Numbers like "Medicube #5 globally / +36 spots" and "TikTok K-beauty SOV 18% → 34%" are presented as 'NEW quantitative anchors' on a single-routine source without secondary verification. Risk: these get cited to outreach contacts (Mamonde, Beauty Vista, Seoul Glow) and the routine's epistemic reputation depends on numbers it has not independently checked.
